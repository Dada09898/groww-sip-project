Before you change your SIP, check in.

A prototype of a check-in that opens when a first-time investor taps Cancel SIP or sees a negative return. Ask in your own words, in English or Hinglish. You get a plain explanation and every option you have. The choice stays yours.

How each answer is checked before you see it

  1. Rules first. Any fund name, return prediction or pressure word blocks the answer.
  2. A second check by the platform. It looks for advice, invented numbers or pushing you to decide.
  3. If either check fails, you see a fixed safe answer with links to AMFI and SEBI instead.

This is not investment advice. It explains your options using your SIP details and doesn’t recommend any fund or stock. For personal advice, talk to a SEBI-registered investment adviser. Learn more at AMFI investor corner. Your message is processed by a third-party AI service, so don’t type account numbers or other personal details.
